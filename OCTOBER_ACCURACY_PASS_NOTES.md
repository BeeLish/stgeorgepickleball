## 1. Little Valley address was wrong on the site and is now corrected

**Before:** 2330 Horseman Park Drive, St. George, UT 84790 — on the homepage directory, the venue page, and both event pages.
**After:** **2149 E Horseman Park Drive, St. George, UT 84790.**

**Why it changed — official sources checked October 7, 2026:**

| Source | Address given |
|---|---|
| City of St. George official pickleball page — "City of St. George Pickleball Courts: Little Valley / 2149 Horseman Park Drive / Courts: 33" | 2149 Horseman Park Drive |
| City of St. George parks page — "The Fields at Little Valley" | 2995 South 2350 East Street (the same park's other frontage) |
| Official Fall Brawl 2026 registration listing | 2149 Horseman Park Dr, St George, UT, 84790 |
| Official Huntsman World Senior Games pickleball page — venue link | 2149 Horseman Park Dr, St. George, UT 84790 |
| Google Maps / Apple Maps / Waze place record | 2149 E Horseman Park Dr |

**Where 2330 came from and what it actually is:** the City's own January 2024 press release announced the expansion at "2330 Horseman Park Drive," and that number was copied onward by the Greater Zion tourism listing and several third-party directories. 2330 E Horseman Park Drive is **Little Valley Elementary School**, a Washington County School District campus next door — the school's own site and the district's elementary school directory both use that address, and mapping data places the school there, east of the court complex.

**Effect on a visitor before this fix:** entering the site's published address into a navigation app would route to a school parking lot roughly a few hundred yards from the courts.

**Wayfinding added to the venue page (all from the City's own parks page):**
- The 33 pickleball courts are on the **west side** of The Fields at Little Valley, closest to Horseman Park Drive.
- The soccer fields, two playgrounds, and splash pad are on the eastern portion of the same complex, which the City lists under the park address 2995 South 2350 East.
- Parking is free on site.

**Also corrected / newly sourced on that page:** hours guidance (the City publishes no official court hours; free public play whenever City programs are not using the courts), amenities now drawn from the City parks listing (restrooms, drinking fountains, parking, covered pavilions, picnic tables, playground and splash pad, court lighting), and a City Parks contact for court reservations — (435) 627-4530.

**Court count (33) confirmed, and the "24" figure explained:** the City's own 2020 Public Facility of the Year text and its October 2024 social post both say 24 courts — that is the pre-expansion count. The City's current schedule tables reserve courts numbered up to 33, and the City's pickleball page states 33.

## 3. Huntsman World Senior Games pickleball 2026 — schedule verified, contradiction still live on the official page

**Verified:** competition Monday, October 12 through Saturday, October 17, 2026. Play begins 8:00 a.m. daily; athlete check-in 7:00 a.m. No make-up days. The official page still marks the schedule **preliminary**.

| Date | Divisions (official day-by-day list) |
|---|---|
| Sun, Oct 11 | Open practice day — all day at Little Valley; SunRiver from 10:00 a.m. |
| Mon, Oct 12 | Men's skill doubles 4.0 / 4.5 / 5.0; women's skill doubles 3.5 / 4.0 / 4.5 / 5.0 |
| Tue, Oct 13 | Men's age doubles; women's 3.0 skill doubles |
| Wed, Oct 14 | Women's age doubles; men's 3.0 and 3.5 skill doubles |
| Thu, Oct 15 | Mixed doubles — ages 50, 55, 60 |
| Fri, Oct 16 | Mixed doubles — ages 65, 70, 75, 80+ |
| Sat, Oct 17 | Men's and women's singles |

**The open-practice contradiction is real and unresolved in the source.** The official sports page puts "Sunday, Oct. 11 — Open Practice Day — All Day" in its day-by-day schedule, but a separate paragraph further down the same page says "Little Valley October 12th, ALL DAY." The official tournament/registration listing independently says Sunday, October 11 at Little Valley with SunRiver open after 10 a.m. The page reports October 11 as the current listing, states the conflict explicitly, and flags the row for the reader to recheck. It does not pick a side silently.

**Additional verified detail added to the page:** athlete ID badge required at venue check-in before play each day; $20 per event sport fee with referee fee included; early-bird deadline August 1, final registration deadline September 1 (both passed); bracket details are not published until the day before each event; age doubles / age mixed doubles / age singles qualify for the 2027 National Senior Games but skill doubles does not; yellow Franklin X-40 balls; the free venue shuttle is an athlete service requiring 90 minutes' notice, not a spectator service; and SunRiver is a private community with its own rules (no dogs except certified service dogs; under-18s must be accompanied and supervised).

**Where the 2026 event stands:** registration closed.
